I am a Ph.D. candidate at [Nanyang Technological University](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/), Singapore. My research focuses on serverless systems, including autoscaling, cluster management, and snapshot state management.

I work on making cloud infrastructure more efficient and responsive. My research includes **PulseNet**, a cluster manager that combines fast scaling for Function as a Service (FaaS) with the feature support of conventional cluster managers, and **SnapShare**, which explores isolation-preserving deduplication for serverless snapshots.

I help maintain the [ARIES agentic experimentation platform](https://github.com/hyscale-lab/ARIES) and [vHive serverless platform](https://github.com/vhive-serverless/vHive), contributing to release management, performance, reliability, and contributor onboarding.

Before my Ph.D., I spent two years as a software intern in **Intel’s Core Microarchitecture Team**, analyzing cache prefetching and cache structures and improving a microprocessor simulator. I hold a **B.Sc. with Honours in Applied Math and Physics** from the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology (MIPT).

My programming experience includes **Go, C++, and Python**, with a background in cloud computing, computer architecture, and performance analysis.
